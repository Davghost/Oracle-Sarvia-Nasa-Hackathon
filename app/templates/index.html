    The clouds hid the flood

    Southern Mozambique, January 2026.

    Heavy rain flooded the Limpopo and Incomati basins. Optical satellites could not see the water: NASA reports that Sentinel-2 saw only clouds while the flood was happening.

    Radar sees through clouds

    NISAR sends its own signal and listens for the echo. Clouds do not stop it, day or night. Calm water acts like a mirror and sends the signal away, so water looks dark.

    The map shows the water the radar found on the first date: rivers, lakes and the sea.
